📊 Normal vs Per 90

For relevant performance statistics, FPL Edge will support both:

Normal Stats  ↔  Per 90 Stats

For example:

Goals ↔ Goals/90
Assists ↔ Assists/90
xG ↔ xG/90
xA ↔ xA/90
Shots ↔ Shots/90
Big chances ↔ Big chances/90
Big chances created ↔ Big chances created/90
Touches in opposition box ↔ Touches in opposition box/90
